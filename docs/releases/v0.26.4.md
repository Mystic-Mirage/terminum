# v0.26.4 — what you copy from the terminal says what it is

A patch release: the leftovers of v0.26.3's review of copying from the terminal. Nothing new to
install; `terminum upgrade` brings it.

## Fixed

- **Copy says what it takes.** The selection's Copy has the tooltip "Copy 3 lines: <its first
  line>", and every Copy's toast says the same once copied ("Copied 3 lines: …"). A program's
  copy is not drawn as it is, so this is the only look at it before you paste; Ask and [+] show
  it whole.
- **Terminal text loses what hides what it runs, wherever it leaves the page.** Copy, ⌘C, [+]
  and Ask, over the terminal's rows and over a rendered widget, now drop bidi controls,
  zero-width characters, the soft hyphen, word joiners, line separators and tag characters, as a
  program's copy already did; ZWNJ and ZWJ stay for words and emoji. When something was left
  out the toast says so: "(2 hidden characters left out)".
- **The bookmark dialog shows what it keeps.** A copy longer than a bookmark's 2,000 characters
  showed whole (a program's copy can be 1 MB) and was cut on saving; the dialog now shows the
  first 2,000 characters, never half an emoji, and says the rest is not kept.
- **One rule for those characters in terminumd.** Names, titles and folders put on tmux command
  lines, agents' plans and records, link addresses in terminal output and a program's copies
  now share one list, where three lists of their own covered it in part: a name with a soft
  hyphen or a word joiner is refused with the usual message, and a line separator in a plan is
  a line end.

## Verified

applied: one `clean` in terminumd for copies and plans, no second name for the rule, the preview
with the text rules in `seltext.js`, the bookmark limit one constant) and a pragmatic review (its
conditions applied: the preview in one linear pass on a hostile 1 MB copy, cleaning at every way
out, tag characters, a test that keeps the page's list and terminumd's equal): `make test`,
clippy, the Panel's browser check, the Page's browser checks (Chromium and WebKit, a wide and a
phone size, and the selection's check in Firefox), `web/test/e2e_live.mjs` and
`scripts/security_check.sh` pass. The toast looked at in Chromium and WebKit, wide and phone, dark
and light.

Not verified: a physical iPhone. Noticed, not fixed: the bookmark limit is still the page's own
2000 rather than terminumd's; other texts terminumd keeps (hot keys' labels, settings, restored
titles) still drop only control characters; they are shown as text, never typed.
