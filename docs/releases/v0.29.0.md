# v0.29.0 — Every bookmark in one list, reminders said when they come up, a counter

A minor release: the list below the sessions changes what it holds and reminders that come up
are now said in a toast (new behavior), with small changes to the Tip of the day and the
bookmarks' window. `terminum upgrade` brings it.

## New

- **Reminders that come up say so** at the terminal's top for 4 seconds: "iQuantize: 16
  reminders, 3 new" (outside projects, the session's title). Reminders that came up in another
  session's project are said when a session of it is shown next. Each reminder is said once;
  the bell stays filled.
- **Settings' "Show today's tip"** brings back a Tip of the day closed with ×; it is there only
  while today's tip is closed.

## Changed

- **Bookmarks / Reminders below the sessions lists them all**, newest first, each with its
  project or session, "closed", "orphaned" or "out of reach"; it listed only those no session
  showed. A session's own panes (its bookmarks' button and the bell) still list only its
  project's records, or its own outside projects.
- **The bookmarks' window counts its entries**: "1 / 52" at the right of each entry's
  where-and-when line, in the same type.
- **The chosen bookmark's title and its text** are the window's size, as the list's titles (the
  title was larger, a bookmark's text in code smaller).
- **The Tip of the day** has a card of its own, so it stands out from the list; Turn off at the
  left, Next at the right, a little space over them; a tip about Settings and its color schemes.
- **On a phone the bookmark bar** after a jump spans the terminal under ⟨…⟩, its keys wrap
  inside it at 320 px, and Quote in chat says "Quote"; it stood in a narrow column beside ⟨…⟩
  with its Delete over the floating buttons.
