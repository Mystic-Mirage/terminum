# v0.26.3 — a full-screen program's copy shows its buttons, whatever is on the screen

A patch release: in Claude Code's fullscreen mode Copy, [+] and Ask still failed to come after
some drags, because the page compared what Claude copied with the screen. Nothing new to install;
`terminum upgrade` brings it.

## Fixed

- **The buttons come for every copy the program makes after your drag.** The page took a
  program's copy only when its lines were on the screen; Claude Code leaves out what it draws
  unselectable (⏺, ⎿) and scrolls its transcript when a drag reaches the top or bottom, so the
  comparison kept failing. It also guarded little: it compared text with the whole screen, so
  content that can write the clipboard had only to print its text first. Now the page shows what
  the program copied, as it copied it.
- **A program's copy carries nothing that hides what it runs.** Besides control characters,
  terminumd now takes bidi controls, zero-width characters and line separators out of it (ZWNJ
  and ZWJ stay for words and emoji), the same rule as for what the Files panel types into a
  terminal.

Unchanged guards: a copy is taken only within 2 s of this page's mouse release over that pane,
and nothing reaches the clipboard or an agent until you press a button. Copy puts the program's
text on the clipboard without showing it; Ask and [+] show it first (`SECURITY.md`).

## Verified

At release, on the delivered tree after `/simplify` over every change since v0.26.2 (its fixes
applied: one rule for the characters that hide what a line runs, shared with what the Files panel
types; one clipboard spy for the checks; shorter comments): `make test`, clippy, the Panel's
browser check, the Page's browser checks (Chromium and WebKit, a wide and a phone size, and the
selection's check in Firefox; with the comparison back, Copy and [+] hold an earlier copy and fail), `web/test/e2e_live.mjs`
and `scripts/security_check.sh` pass. A pragmatic review approved dropping the comparison; its
wording for `SECURITY.md` and the character rule are applied. Live against a private terminumd and
tmux with Claude Code 2.1.316 in fullscreen mode, in Chromium and Firefox: 12 drags held at the
top row all showed the buttons, one copying 54 lines from a 50-row screen; 32 of 32 two-row drags
did; the owner's sequence of drags, Shift and a click held.

Not verified: other Claude Code versions, a physical iPhone. Unchanged: Claude copies nothing for
its logo, blank cells and `/context`'s legend beside its grid, so no buttons come there; a browser
selection's Copy is not cleaned of bidi characters (only a program's copy is).
