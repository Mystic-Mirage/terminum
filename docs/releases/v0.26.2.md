# v0.26.2 — selecting in Claude Code's fullscreen mode works everywhere on its screen

A patch release: in Claude Code's fullscreen mode Copy, [+] and Ask came for a few drags, then not
in another part of the screen, and after a Shift selection and a click nothing worked for a while.
Nothing new to install; `terminum upgrade` brings it.

## Fixed

- **A drag over Claude Code's replies and tool output shows the buttons.** Claude Code leaves out
  of what it copies the marks it draws unselectable (⏺ before its reply, ⎿ before a command's
  output, its logo), so a copy over such rows was not one piece of the screen and the page dropped
  it. Now each line of what it copied must be on the screen.
- **A click after a Shift selection works again.** The click went to the program but the browser's
  Shift selection stayed: the terminal did not get the keys back, and that old selection could
  stand in for what the program copied next. A press that goes to the program now ends the
  browser's selection, as a click in a terminal does.

## Verified

At release, on the delivered tree after `/simplify` over every change since v0.26.1 (its fixes
applied: a copy longer than the screen's text dropped at once, shorter comments, the check's rows
and redraws written once): `make test`, the Panel's browser check, the Page's browser checks (Chromium and WebKit, a wide and a phone size, and the selection's check
in Firefox; the two new checks fail with the fix undone), `web/test/e2e_live.mjs` and
`scripts/security_check.sh` pass. The owner's sequence by hand against a private terminumd and
tmux with Claude Code 2.1.316 in fullscreen mode, in Chromium and Firefox: drags in several places,
Shift and a drag, a click, drags again; then 31 drags over two rows each across `/context`'s output
(⎿, ⛁, └): the buttons came for every one, the keys reached Claude after the click, and [+] held
Claude's copy.

Not verified: other Claude Code versions, a physical iPhone. Unchanged: a browser selection in an
agent's input box or status lines shows no buttons (AC-57); a copy reaching past the rows on the
screen (a drag that scrolled Claude's transcript) shows none either.
